Women Welfare Programs

Self Defense Training

Free self-defense training programs help women learn techniques to protect themselves in dangerous situations.

Women Safety Awareness

Workshops and awareness campaigns are conducted in schools and colleges to educate women about their rights and safety.

Government Support Schemes

Government initiatives provide financial support, education, and protection services for women.

Legal Help and Counseling

Women can receive free legal advice and counseling services to help them report crimes and get justice.

Emergency Helpline Services

Women can contact emergency helplines for immediate help in unsafe situations.

Women Helpline: 181 | Police: 100 | Ambulance: 108
